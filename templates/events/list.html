We're here to help ↗
How do I get my ticket?+

Once payment is confirmed, your QR tickets appear on the confirmation page. If you provide an email, we'll send a copy there too. You can retrieve them anytime using your order reference and phone number under My tickets.

Do I need an account to book?+

No. Guest checkout is always available. Create an account if you'd like to keep your purchases together in one place.

Can I pay with M-Pesa?+

Yes. Enter your Safaricom number at checkout and confirm the payment prompt on your phone. Your PIN is entered only on your phone, never on this website.

What if my plans change?+

Refund eligibility follows the event's policy and our Terms of Service. Contact our team with your order reference so we can help with your booking.
